SapoCalc – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

In short: SapoCalc has no user accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your recipes, batches and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any server and we do not receive any of your data. The only data that leaves your device is limited technical diagnostics sent to Google by the on-device text recognition component (Google ML Kit), described in section 4.

1. Who we are

SapoCalc is a soap and lye calculator for Android, developed by Dev_App_IT (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy, write to alessio2793@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • recipes, notes, folders, tags and ratings;
  • batches, cure dates and weight records;
  • custom oils and ingredient prices you enter;
  • app settings (language, units, theme, defaults);
  • references to photos you attach to recipes or batches.

We do not collect, upload or have access to any of this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

4. Reading recipes from photos (Google ML Kit)

To read a recipe from a photo, SapoCalc uses Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your device. The photo and the recognized text are not sent to Google or to us.

Like all ML Kit features, the component sends Google limited technical information to measure performance, debug, maintain and improve the service and detect misuse, for example:

  • device information: manufacturer, model, operating system version and build;
  • app information: package name and app version;
  • a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device;
  • performance metrics (such as processing time), image format and size, feature version and error codes.

According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. It is processed by Google under the ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it.

5. Ratings and reviews

After you have saved a few recipes, SapoCalc may show the standard Google Play rating dialog. The dialog and your review are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; we only see public reviews in the Play Store, like everyone else.

6. Sharing, PDF and backups

When you share a recipe (image, text or PDF) or save a backup file, SapoCalc creates the file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The data goes only to the app or place you choose (for example a messaging app, email or cloud storage) and is then governed by that service’s privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

7. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking by us, and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No collection of payment information (the app is free and has no in-app purchases).

8. Children

SapoCalc is intended for adults. Soap making involves caustic chemicals (sodium and potassium hydroxide). The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. You control all data in the app and can delete it at any time by deleting recipes and batches, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google (sections 4 and 5), see the Google Privacy Policy. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also contact us or your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the new version of the app is released.
