Spoolbook – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

In short: Spoolbook has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your filaments, spools, printers, jobs, quotes and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any server and we do not receive any of your data. Purchases of Spoolbook Pro are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Spoolbook is a 3D print cost calculator and filament inventory app for Android, developed by Dev_App_IT (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy, write to alessio2793@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • filaments, spools, printers, their prices and settings;
  • print jobs, costs, client names and notes you type;
  • business details and the logo you choose for your quotes;
  • app settings (currency, language, theme, defaults).

We do not collect, upload or have access to any of this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Files you open

When you import a slicer file (G-code, 3MF, bgcode, UFP) or restore a backup, you choose the file with the Android file picker. Spoolbook reads it on your device to fill in print time, grams and filaments. The file is not uploaded anywhere and is not kept by the app.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Photos (system photo picker)Only when you choose an image to use as the logo on your quotes. Android’s photo picker gives the app only the image you select; Spoolbook has no access to the rest of your gallery.
InternetSpoolbook does not use the internet itself. The connection is used by Google Play to sell and verify the Spoolbook Pro purchase and to show the rating dialog (sections 5 and 6).

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, accounts or storage.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Spoolbook Pro is a one-time in-app purchase sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google: we never see your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Spoolbook Pro has been purchased on your Google account, in order to unlock Pro features on your device. Google processes purchase data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. As the developer, Google Play gives us the standard sales reports of every Play developer (for example order numbers, amounts and country); we use them only for accounting and refunds.

6. Ratings and reviews

After you have saved a few jobs, Spoolbook may show the standard Google Play rating dialog. The dialog and your review are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; we only see public reviews in the Play Store, like everyone else.

7. Sharing quotes, CSV exports and backups

When you share a PDF quote, export your jobs to CSV or save a backup file, Spoolbook creates the file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The data goes only to the app or place you choose (for example email, a messaging app or cloud storage) and is then governed by that service’s privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

8. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking by us, and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No cloud sync: your data never leaves your device unless you share or back it up yourself.

9. Children

Spoolbook is a tool for makers and small businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. You control all data in the app and can delete it at any time by deleting items, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google (sections 5 and 6), see the Google Privacy Policy. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also contact us or your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the new version of the app is released.
